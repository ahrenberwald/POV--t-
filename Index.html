About

Project Description

The intent of this project is to develop a tool for the U of S Women's Soccer Team to monitor what athletes focus on during games. This tool will provide data on players' focus that can be reviewed after training. It works for many players at once without hindering their abilities while providing usability for both indoor and outdoor sessions.
